# Bousai Guide

Bousai Guide helps residents of Japan understand mapped hazards relevant to a home or neighbourhood.

## Language

**Location assessment**:
Inspection and interpretation of mapped hazards for a home or neighbourhood, including the limits of the information and an official source for further checking.
_Avoid_: Safety verdict, complete risk assessment

**Unshaded area**:
An area with no visible hazard shading in the information being inspected. Absence of shading is not proof of safety.
_Avoid_: Safe area, hazard-free area
